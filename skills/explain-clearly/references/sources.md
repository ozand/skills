# Sources and limits

These are practitioner articles informing parts of the writing approach. They are not
controlled studies, and this skill does not claim they establish improved human
comprehension. The skill adapts selected advice for a broad, non-specialist audience;
it is not a mandatory checklist or a claim of endorsement.

- Julia Evans, [Patterns in confusing explanations](https://jvns.ca/blog/confusing-explanations/)
- Julia Evans, [Teaching by filling in knowledge gaps](https://jvns.ca/blog/2021/09/20/teaching-by-filling-in-knowledge-gaps/)
- Julia Evans, [Write good examples by starting with real code](https://jvns.ca/blog/2021/07/08/writing-great-examples/)
- Julia Evans, [Using `make` to compile C programs (for non-C-programmers)](https://jvns.ca/blog/2025/06/10/how-to-compile-a-c-program/)

No Feynman, ITIL, or Agile provenance is asserted: those attributions are not
established by the source material used for this package.
